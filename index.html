About AVIO X

An independent drone and machine workshop in Lithuania. Every build is designed and made from zero for one specific job.

What I build

  • Fixed-wing UAVs
  • Nature-inspired and flapping-wing drones
  • RC vehicles and mechanisms
  • Electronics and control: ESP32, telemetry, live camera

How it works

  1. You describe the job.
  2. You get a fixed price and a date before anything starts.
  3. I design, build and test, and send progress videos.
  4. You get the finished build.

Good to know

Flying rules depend on the drone's weight and where you fly. I'll tell you what probably applies, but check with your aviation authority.

Languages: Lithuanian, English.
