01 — Disciplines

Where biology, aerodynamics and electronics meet.

Nature solved flight at tiny scales long before we did. AvioX copies the useful parts — veined membranes, flapping strokes, low mass — and backs them with real engineering.

Aerodynamics

Wings that work

Fixed wings, flying wings and flapping membranes. Control surfaces shift the pressure across a wing to steer it; flapping wings make lift and thrust in the same stroke.

Electronics

Nervous systems

ESP32 boards, flight controllers, video links and custom firmware. Control over WiFi, telemetry and live camera — packed into grams.

Mechanisms

Printed gear trains

Involute spur gears printed at module 0.6–0.8, carbon rods, hand-tuned linkages — reduction stages small enough to sit beside a coin.
